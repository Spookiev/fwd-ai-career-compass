# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## 1. Project Identification & Academic Metadata
- **Project Name:** FWD (Forward) — Forward with Skills, Forward with Career
- **Problem Statement ID:** PSAIAC_36 (*AI Assistant for Personalized Career Pathway Exploration in Universities*)
- **Course:** CSS7102 – Mini Project
- **Target Audience:** University Students, Placement Cells, Academic & Career Advisors
- **Brand Motto:** *"Accelerate Your Potential. Step Forward with Confidence."*
- **SDG Alignment:** 
  - **SDG 4 (Quality Education):** Personalized, open-access career guidance and skill mapping.
  - **SDG 8 (Decent Work & Economic Growth):** Enhancing student employability and placement readiness.

---

## 2. Executive Summary & Problem Definition
University students face high career uncertainty due to generic advice, a lack of progress-driven tracking, and no unified bridge between aptitude performance and real-world hiring criteria.

**FWD** is an intelligent, multi-factor career guidance and skill progression platform. It integrates:
1. Automated PDF resume ingestion and skill inventorying.
2. Diagnostic skill & aptitude benchmarking.
3. Transparent AI-driven career path matching via Gemini AI.
4. Skill-gap diagnostics against live industry benchmarks.
5. Tiered, milestone-based learning roadmaps curated with verified free Open Educational Resources (NPTEL, YouTube, Coursera Audit, Open Books).
6. Placement preparation tools (ATS resume auditing, mock interview simulations).
7. Institutional governance via a dedicated Faculty Mentorship Portal.
8. Workload regulation and burnout prevention.

---

## 3. User Personas & Core User Journeys

### Persona A: The Targeted Student (Has Existing Resume)
- **Flow:** Ingests PDF Resume $\rightarrow$ Automatic skill extraction $\rightarrow$ Selects target role $\rightarrow$ Runs Skill Gap Diagnostic $\rightarrow$ Receives personalized roadmap & placement prep $\rightarrow$ Tracks workload.

### Persona B: The Exploring Student (Uncertain Career Pathway)
- **Flow:** Selects Domain of Interest $\rightarrow$ Takes 5-minute Diagnostic Skill & Aptitude Assessment $\rightarrow$ Gemini AI computes Top-5 Career Matches with transparent explainability $\rightarrow$ Adopts recommended roadmap.

### Persona C: The Faculty Advisor / Placement Mentor
- **Flow:** Logs into Faculty Hub $\rightarrow$ Reviews mentee progress & CGPA $\rightarrow$ Approves roadmap milestones & internship credits $\rightarrow$ Posts official endorsements and confidential advisory notes.

## 4. Functional Requirements & Feature Specifications

### Module 1: Dual-Track Authentication & Profile Matrix
- **FR-1.1:** Authentication via Firebase Auth (Google OAuth 2.0 and Email/Password with verification).
- **FR-1.2:** Profile intake supporting full academic metadata (CGPA, semester, university, major, graduation year).
- **FR-1.3:** Resume ingestion supporting client-side parsing of PDF/DOCX via `pdfjs-dist` to populate programming languages, frameworks, and projects automatically.

### Module 2: Diagnostic Skill & Aptitude Calibration
- **FR-2.1:** Dynamic 5-minute diagnostic assessment covering quantitative aptitude, logical reasoning, and domain technical logic.
- **FR-2.2:** Automated classification into performance tiers: `Foundation Tier` (Beginner), `Associate Tier` (College/Intermediate), or `Advanced Tier` (Industry Ready).
- **FR-2.3:** Realistic timeline calculation (3-month, 6-month, or 9-month paths) based on baseline scores.

### Module 3: Transparent AI Career Match Engine
- **FR-3.1:** Multi-factor analysis via Gemini AI generating Top 5 Matched Careers with strict explainability rationale (why the student fits the role based on coursework, aptitude, and skills).
- **FR-3.2:** Explicit breakdown of candidate strengths, areas to develop, and priority missing skills.

### Module 4: Skill Gap Matrix & Tiered Learning Roadmap
- **FR-4.1:** Side-by-side delta table comparing student profile against industry benchmark prerequisites.
- **FR-4.2:** Roadmap structured chronologically into Phases, Monthly Goals, Weekly Tasks, and Capstone Projects.
- **FR-4.3:** Free resource curation linking directly to NPTEL/SWAYAM, YouTube playlists, Coursera audit courses, and book summaries.
- **FR-4.4:** Interactive task checking with dynamic readiness score recalculation and confetti triggers.

### Module 5: ATS Resume Auditor & Quantifier
- **FR-5.1:** Resume scoring out of 100 with category grades (Impact, Brevity, Style, Skills).
- **FR-5.2:** Missing ATS keyword detector with 1-click clipboard copy.
- **FR-5.3:** AI-quantified bullet-point rewrite suggestions comparing original vs. improved statements with recruiter rationale.

### Module 6: Multi-Track AI Interview Simulator
- **FR-6.1:** Role-based interview question generator across 4 categories: `HR`, `Technical`, `Coding Logic`, and `Behavioral`.
- **FR-6.2:** Built-in code implementation playground with starter code and test case specifications.
- **FR-6.3:** Instant grading by Gemini evaluating STAR method adherence, Big-O complexity, and model answers.

### Module 7: Live Job Market Analytics & Direct Application Gateway
- **FR-7.1:** Interactive visual dashboards (Top hiring skills demand %, multi-year tech momentum 2024–2027, salary progression by experience).
- **FR-7.2:** "Ready to Apply" status gating: locks direct job apply links until student readiness score meets minimum threshold ($>75\%$).

### Module 8: Workload Regulation & Burnout Prevention
- **FR-8.1:** Workload tracking monitoring streak duration and task completion velocity.
- **FR-8.2:** Fatigue alert engine that automatically throttles daily targets and recommends scheduled breaks when overload patterns emerge.

### Module 9: Faculty Mentorship & Institutional Hub
- **FR-9.1:** Mentee directory with live CGPA, readiness index, and roadmap completion metrics.
- **FR-9.2:** Approvals workflow for roadmap submissions, internship credit exemptions, and track changes.
- **FR-9.3:** Structured feedback posting with star ratings, action items, and private/shared advisory notes.

---

## 5. Non-Functional Requirements
- **Performance:** LLM inference response times $\le 2.5\text{s}$; client-side PDF parsing $\le 1.0\text{s}$.
- **Security:** Zero plaintext API key storage; Firebase Security Rules ensuring student data isolation; confidential faculty notes hidden from student access.
- **Reliability:** Offline-first caching of active roadmaps and checklists via Firestore local persistence.
- **Academic Governance:** Exportable PDF summaries of student roadmaps and faculty endorsements for university placement audits.
