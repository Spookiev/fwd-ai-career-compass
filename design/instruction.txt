2. Design Philosophy & Visual Aesthetic
Theme: Vibrant Neo-Glassmorphism with deep purple-to-magenta ambient mesh gradient.

Visual Metaphor: "The Student Pro Card" — Treats each student as a high-performing talent building career stats, tracking placement metrics, and advancing tiers through clean data cards.

Key UI Signatures:

Dynamic Visuals: Left-aligned persona visual with tier numbers.

Floating Frosted Glassmorphism: Translucent slate/purple panels with soft blur (backdrop-blur-2xl), rounded contours (rounded-4xl), and subtle 1px border glows.

Tri-Color KPI Pill Badges: Bold high-contrast metric boxes (Coral Red, Deep Blue, Pure White) for immediate visual hierarchy.

Central Fluid/Organic Radar: Fluid concentric competency radar mapping aptitude, technical depth, and soft skills.

Clean Capsule Floating Navigation: Centered top pill dock with minimalist micro-icons.
